Best-fit Gaussian amplitude (fixed σ) vs pixel

Fixed-width (σ = 70.7 ps, the cross-node TDC instrument width) Gaussian fit to peak height, as % of baseline — back from the free-σ peak-area metric used 24-9-26–27-9-26: area/σ is a nonlinear ratio of two parameters that a free-σ fit can't independently pin down once the peak (FWHM ≈ 166 ps) spans only ~1.7 of these 100 ps bins, so amplitude and fitted σ are strongly correlated under this binning; fixing σ sidesteps that degeneracy at the cost of undercounting area on runs whose peak is genuinely broadened (long-run clock drift). Poisson errors, validated against 3,063 off-peak null fits (pull RMS 0.99). Points trimmed by the active cut are still drawn, greyed out, for context — they never enter the mean, χ²/dof or trend line. Static analysis — regenerate via tools/g2_analysis/ after new sweep data; last run 27-9-26 on the full 97-result set. Full write-up: tools/g2_analysis/CLAUDE.md.

Split-peak cut (28-9-26, no SNR cut): all 97 results were searched with a 70.7 ps matched filter. A result is split when a second bunching component with MF-SNR ≥ 3.5 sits at any whole multiple of ±100 ns (±0.3 ns) from the best matched-filter peak — the signature of the 100 ns timing jumps seen in the px 151 reliability runs, which divide the signal and bias the single-peak amplitude low (~1 false flag expected over the whole sweep). 10 results qualify (123 rep, 139, 141, 143 rep, 147, 147 rep, 159, 159 rep, 165 rep, 175 rep); they are flagged split_peak in the database, drawn as grey open squares and excluded. Every other result is kept, including non-detections, so χ²/dof here is dominated by low-SNR fits.

Repeat reliability check · px 151 & 164

Up to 50 independent 20-min repeats, both pixels active simultaneously (identity pairs, one full lSPAD reset + recalibration between repeats, same as the main sweep). Motivation: 151 has shown inconsistent bunching across separate runs (present but at varying τ/strength, sometimes absent); 164 has looked rock solid every time. This tab exists to answer empirically whether the point-to-point scatter here matches the quoted Poisson error bars, or whether 151 is genuinely unstable. Each repeat is pushed live as its round finishes (fixed-σ amplitude fit, same convention as the main panel); the weighted mean/χ²/dof below update as repeats arrive — a χ²/dof near 1 means the scatter is explained by the quoted errors, »1 means it isn't.

Rounds flagged excluded in the database are drawn greyed out and do not enter the mean or χ²/dof. Rule (applied 28-9-26): every round below the SNR 5.7 cut was searched with a 70.7 ps matched filter at all 100 ns timing-ladder positions; split = bunching found at ≥2 positions (MF-SNR ≥ 3), i.e. a timing jump during the run divided the signal; lost = only one weak component, most signal missing. Weak rounds with a single clean peak would have been kept — none occurred. px 151: 7 split + 2 lost of 50; px 164: none.

px 151 with live timing-jump correction · 50 × 20 min

The px 151 reliability sweep, repeated with the master-chip correction running live. Pixel 151 sits on the master chip, whose clock can jump by a whole 100 ns tick against the slave chip in the middle of a run; that is what split or lost 9 of the 50 earlier rounds. Here a dwell-marker tracker watches each node's master−slave offset, and the correlator removes a confirmed jump from the 151×151 time differences from that moment on (data already released stays as it was). Pixel 0, a slave-chip pixel, is kept active on both nodes only to supply the slave dwell markers; it is not correlated. Everything else matches the main sweep: 100 ps bins, 20-minute rounds, a full lSPAD reset and recalibration before each round, fixed-σ (70.7 ps) amplitude fit. Every round is shown; a round with jumps is not dropped. Only a round whose tracker never found a level (correction not running) is greyed out and left out of the mean. Each round is added about five minutes after it ends, drawn like the other tabs: the raw histogram, ±200 ns around its peak.

pLEE (shown on each card) is a look-elsewhere-corrected p-value: the local Poisson p-value of the tallest bin in the full ±500 ns / 10,001-bin window, corrected for that many trials (pLEE = 1−(1−plocal)10001). A peak is counted as significant when its SNR ≥ 5.7. Each card shows the full ±200 ns window at native 100 ps resolution; amplitude is a fixed-width Gaussian fit (σ = 70.7 ps, the cross-node TDC instrument width) over a ±2 ns window around the tallest bin, reported as % of baseline ± 1σ fit uncertainty.
